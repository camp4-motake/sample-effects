One crystal, glowing beneath the dark

Light begins in
the deepest stillness.

Hyalite is a crystal-shaped light made to float above a shallow bowl of water in a dark room. It isn’t there to brighten the space, but to be watched: only the ripples it touches come into view, and everything else dissolves into black.

Crystal
A hexagonal prism closed by six-sided points at both ends, the shape of natural quartz. Each facet returns light differently, so it seems to breathe as it glows.
Surface
The faintest draft keeps the water moving. What you see is only the passing shape of each ripple that catches the crystal’s light.
Perspective
Lower your eyes to the waterline and the band of light stretches out, while the crystal seems to hover overhead.
